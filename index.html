Our Story

Built in Defense. Delivered for Government.

RA Federal Solutions was born from a belief that the precision required to engineer aircraft systems is the same precision government agencies deserve in every engagement — from large-scale conferences to workforce training and technical projects.

Our founder, Busola Kalejaiye, brings an Aerospace Engineering degree and firsthand experience as a Systems Engineer at some of the nation's most respected defense and aviation contractors — BAE Systems, General Dynamics, and Rockwell Collins. That foundation instilled a discipline for structure, risk management, and zero-margin-for-error execution that defines everything we do.

Today, that same defense-program rigor drives a full-service professional firm serving state and federal agencies — with a track record spanning enterprise program management at Blue Cross Blue Shield, Delta, and Long Term Care Group, and compliance-critical systems training for healthcare professionals across multiple organizations.

We don't just plan events and deliver training. We engineer outcomes.
