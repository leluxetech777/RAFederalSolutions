Company Overview

RA Federal Solutions is a woman-owned professional services firm serving state, federal, and local government agencies from Murrieta, California. We deliver government event planning, professional training and development, and project management consulting — combining the discipline of defense-program experience, enterprise project management, and compliance-critical training delivery to execute every engagement with precision, accountability, and polish.

Core Competencies

  • Government conference, meeting & event management
  • Agency award ceremonies & recognition events
  • Large-scale workforce training & professional development
  • Credentialed training delivery in compliance-critical environments
  • PMI/PMP project & program management
  • Process improvement & operational consulting
  • Procurement liaison & vendor management
  • Hybrid, virtual & in-person event coordination

Past Performance

  • Enterprise program & project management — Blue Cross Blue Shield, Delta, Long Term Care Group
  • Large-scale credentialed training delivery in compliance-driven, regulated environments
  • Full-lifecycle event planning & execution for corporate, social, and professional engagements
  • Operational process improvement & procurement liaison supporting business operations and vendor management

Differentiators

  • Defense-Program Discipline — Systems engineering experience at BAE Systems, General Dynamics & Rockwell Collins brings structured, risk-managed execution to every engagement
  • Government & Compliance Fluency — Deep familiarity with government protocols, chain of command, per diem requirements, and agency compliance standards
  • Enterprise Project Management — Programs managed at Blue Cross Blue Shield, Delta & Long Term Care Group — every engagement delivered with a formal project plan and after-action report
  • Credentialed Trainer — Demonstrated experience delivering large-scale workforce training in compliance-critical, regulated environments
  • Woman-Owned Small Business — Actively pursuing SBA WOSB & EDWOSB certification — supporting agency small business and diversity contracting goals
  • Local California Presence — Based in Murrieta, CA with capacity to serve agencies throughout California and federal clients nationwide
